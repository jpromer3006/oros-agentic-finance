# OROS Longitudinal Validation Protocol
**Version 0.1, pre registration draft**
**Author:** JP Romero
**Date:** August 27, 2026

---

## 0. Why this document exists

Capstone feedback identified rigorous longitudinal validation as the critical next step in turning a functioning system into evidence that the approach consistently delivers value. This is the design for that study, written **before** the results are known so that the results cannot be selected after the fact.

The governing principle is pre registration. Hypotheses, metrics, sample sizes, exclusions and failure criteria are all fixed in this document and hashed. Any change after data collection begins is recorded as a dated amendment rather than an edit.

---

## 1. The claim under test

OROS ranks securities with a regime blended composite drawn from eight peer strategy books, then sizes positions with deterministic logic. The claim to be tested is about **the ranking**, not about any one portfolio.

**H0 (null):** The OROS composite score carries no information about forward excess return once market exposure is removed. Formally, the cross sectional rank correlation between composite score and forward market relative return has expectation zero.

**H1 (alternative):** The composite carries positive information, and that information survives transaction costs, survivorship correction and the exclusion of any single strategy book.

Stating it this way matters. "Did my portfolio beat SPY" is a question about one path. "Does the ranking predict" is a question about thousands of observations, and it is the question that can actually be answered in months rather than decades.

---

## 2. The statistical reality, stated up front

This is the section to put in front of anyone who asks how long validation takes.

**Testing the portfolio is nearly intractable on a personal timeline.** The t statistic of an annualized Sharpe ratio is approximately the Sharpe multiplied by the square root of the number of years observed. To reach conventional significance:

| True Sharpe ratio | Years of forward record needed for t = 1.96 |
|---|---|
| 0.40 | 24.0 |
| 0.50 | 15.4 |
| 0.75 | 6.8 |
| 1.00 | 3.8 |
| 1.50 | 1.7 |

A seven week record sits at roughly 0.13 years. It cannot distinguish a Sharpe of 1.5 from a Sharpe of zero. This is not a criticism of the work, it is arithmetic, and it is the reason the frozen July 6 portfolio moved from 2.9 points ahead of SPY to 3.5 points behind it in 21 trading days without a single change to the holdings.

**Testing the ranking is tractable.** The information coefficient is the Spearman rank correlation between the composite score and forward market relative return, computed within each decision date's cross section and then averaged across dates. Its standard error falls with the number of *dates*, and each date supplies a full cross section.

| True mean IC | Decision dates needed for t = 1.96 (assuming IC volatility 0.15) |
|---|---|
| 0.02 | 216 |
| 0.03 | 96 |
| 0.04 | 54 |
| 0.05 | 35 |

A respectable equity signal lives in the 0.03 to 0.05 band. That means **roughly 4 to 12 months of daily decision dates**, or an equivalent number of historical as of dates generated by the backtest harness. This is achievable, and it is the correct primary endpoint.

**Testing individual trades sits in between.** For a 20 trading day horizon with single name return volatility near 11%, the number of independent trades needed to detect a mean edge:

| Target edge per 20 days | Trades needed |
|---|---|
| 0.5% | 1,859 |
| 1.0% | 465 |
| 1.5% | 207 |
| 2.0% | 116 |

The journal already holds 1,499 scored pick rows across 19 decision dates. The rows are not independent, because picks made on the same day share a market factor, so the effective sample is far smaller than 1,499. This is why every return in this study is measured **relative to a matched market window** and why standard errors are **clustered by decision date**. Reporting raw counts without that correction would be the single easiest way to fool ourselves.

## 3. Study design: three independent tracks

No single track is sufficient. The tracks are designed to fail independently, so that agreement between them is meaningful.

### Track A: Retrospective walk forward backtest
The existing harness (`evaluation/backtest.py`) is methodologically sound and already excludes fundamentals because the only source is a current snapshot that would leak the future. Four corrections are required before results are reportable.

1. **Survivorship correction.** Reconstruct the universe as of each historical date, including securities that were later delisted, acquired or bankrupted. Without this, every historical study manufactures apparent edge by excluding the failures. This is the largest single threat to validity in the entire study.
2. **Transaction costs and slippage.** Model commission, half spread and a volume dependent impact term. Report results at 0, 10, 25 and 50 basis points per side so the cost sensitivity of the edge is visible rather than hidden in one assumption.
3. **Point in time fundamentals.** `ml/fundamentals_pit.py` exists. Extend it so the fundamental books (Classic Value, GARP Growth, and the fundamental half of O'Neil) can enter the study with as reported figures and correct filing lag, rather than being excluded.
4. **Multiple testing correction.** The registry holds eight books, the router holds several regimes, and there are multiple horizons. That is a large search space. Report a deflated Sharpe ratio or an equivalent correction, and declare the number of configurations tried.

**Coverage:** January 2010 through December 2025, spanning at minimum the 2015 and 2018 corrections, the 2020 crash and recovery, the 2022 bear market and the subsequent advance. Report every metric segmented by regime, because a system with a regime router must be shown to work in more than one regime.

### Track B: Forward out of sample record
This track is already accruing and is the only one immune to backtest overfitting.

- **Start of record:** May 26, 2026, the first journaled pick day.
- **Stopping rule, declared now:** the primary readout occurs at 250 decision dates or December 31, 2027, whichever comes first. No interim result is treated as conclusive. No stopping early on a good number.
- **Immutability requirement:** see section 5. The forward record is worthless if it can be edited, and today it can be.

### Track C: Component ablation
The most credible finding available in the near term is not "OROS works." It is "this specific component contributes this specific amount." Ablations run on the same data and the same metric, so differences are attributable.

| Ablation | Question answered |
|---|---|
| Deterministic scorer alone vs full agent pipeline | Does the language model layer add ranking information, or only explanation? |
| Regime router on vs fixed equal weights | Does regime conditioning add value beyond a static blend? |
| Memory tilt on vs off | Does the experience weighting improve or degrade the composite? |
| Each of the eight books alone vs the ensemble | Is the ensemble better than its best member, or is one book carrying it? |
| Entry gates on vs off | Do the volume and extension gates improve risk adjusted outcomes or only reduce turnover? |
| RAG memo with retrieval vs without | Measured on citation validator pass rate and golden question coverage, not on returns |

The agent layer ablation is the one most likely to produce a publishable result, and it is the one most relevant outside finance. If deterministic scoring carries the ranking and the agent layer carries only the explanation, that is a genuinely useful finding about where language models do and do not add value in a decision system, and it generalizes well beyond stock selection.

## 4. Metrics

**Primary endpoint:** mean information coefficient of the composite against 20 trading day forward market relative return, with standard errors clustered by decision date.

**Secondary endpoints:**
- IC decay curve across 1, 5, 10, 20, 60 trading day horizons
- Quintile spread: mean forward excess return of the top scored quintile minus the bottom quintile
- Hit rate against the contemporaneous base rate, not against 50%
- Turnover and cost drag
- Maximum drawdown and time to recovery
- Beta and factor loadings, so that any apparent edge is checked against simple momentum, size, value and low volatility rather than claimed as novel

**Explicitly not a primary endpoint:** total return of any single portfolio. It will be reported for context and labeled as a single path.

## 5. Evidence integrity requirements

A longitudinal claim rests entirely on the reader believing no entry was edited after the outcome was known. Five defects found in the August 27, 2026 audit must be closed first.

1. **Append only ledger with hash chaining.** Each journal entry stores the hash of its predecessor. Any retroactive edit breaks the chain and is detectable by a verification pass that runs on every write.
2. **Atomic writes.** Write to a temporary file and rename. `capstone_chain.json` currently loses its entire history on a single failed parse or partial write, which is how the July 6 chain silently reset to four observations and began reporting an incorrect return since inception.
3. **Reconciliation assertion.** On every run, assert that the number of retained observations is consistent with the declared start date and the trading calendar. Fail loudly rather than reporting a wrong number quietly.
4. **Raw price snapshots at capture time.** The current cache returns different historical closes than it did five weeks ago, because dividend and split adjustments are applied retroactively by the provider. Store raw unadjusted OHLCV as captured, keep adjustment factors as a separate time series, and reconstruct adjusted prices deterministically at analysis time. Without this, no backtest is reproducible and no historical figure is verifiable, which alone would sink a longitudinal claim.
5. **Timestamped external anchoring.** Publish a periodic hash of the ledger somewhere public and time stamped. This converts "trust me, I did not edit it" into something a third party can check.

Additionally: the real money mirror file carries an account identifier and must never enter any repository, report or external document.

## 6. Falsification criteria, declared in advance

The study is only meaningful if a result exists that would cause the approach to be abandoned or materially revised. These are declared now.

- If mean IC is not statistically distinguishable from zero at 250 decision dates, the composite ranking is not adding value and the system should be repositioned as a research and explanation tool rather than a selection tool.
- If the edge disappears entirely at 25 basis points per side of cost, the finding is not economically meaningful regardless of statistical significance.
- If survivorship correction removes more than half of the measured edge, the original result was an artifact.
- If the deterministic scorer alone matches the full agent pipeline on IC, the agent layer is not contributing to selection and should be described honestly as an explanation and provenance layer.
- If the ensemble does not beat its best single book, the ensemble is complexity without benefit.

Roughly four to five months of focused part time work to a first reportable result, with the forward record continuing behind it.

## 8. What can honestly be said before any of this completes

> OROS is instrumented for falsification. Roughly 1,500 decisions have been journaled with entry, stop and target recorded before the outcome was known, a point in time backtest harness exists with documented no lookahead construction, a citation validator mechanically enforces provenance on every numerical claim in every memo, and a frozen benchmark portfolio has been tracked untouched since July 6, 2026. No performance claim is made, and the frozen portfolio's own record moved more than six percentage points relative to the market in twenty one trading days without a single change to its holdings, which is the clearest available demonstration of why a short record proves nothing.

That paragraph is defensible today, in front of anyone.
